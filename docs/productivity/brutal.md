# brutal

## What it does

`brutal` gives a brutally honest verdict on whatever you name — a game, a
server, a GitHub profile, a business: a percent-done number, what reads as AI
slop or amateur work, the one decision it would reverse, and the three fixes to
make first.

It looks before it judges. Every claim points to something it actually ran,
opened or screenshotted, and the bar is the real one for that kind of thing —
shipped games, top servers, funded competitors — not "fine for a side project".

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/brutal` — the agent won't reach for it on its own.
Pass what to judge: `/brutal the game`, `/brutal my github profile`.

Reach for it when you want to know how close something is, or whether it is
good. For what to add next, use [what-else](./what-else.md).

## Common questions

**Does it fix anything?**

No. It ends by offering `/do-it-all` for the fixes.

**Why a percent?**

Because "almost there" hides the gap. The number comes with what 100% means, so
you can argue with the definition rather than the vibe.

## It's working if

- It names specific weak spots with evidence, not general advice.
- The praise is at most three lines.
- Blockers and polish are kept apart.
