# what-else

Answer "what else can we do?" with evidence, not a brainstorm from memory. Do not build anything in this skill.

## 1. Pin the subject

Use the argument, or the project this session is working on. State in one line what it is and what success means for it (members who stay, players who return, paying customers, a finished album).

## 2. Inventory what exists

Read the repo, its docs and its config. Know what is already built so the list never proposes it again.

## 3. Benchmark

Find 3–5 best-in-class examples of the same kind of thing: the top servers, the best-rated games in the genre, the leading products. Use web search; cite each source.

For each, note concrete practice: features, onboarding, the loop that brings people back, presentation, monetization, community.

## 4. Gap table

| # | Idea | What the best do | What we have | Impact | Effort | Who |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Impact and effort: H / M / L.
- Who: **Claude** (doable with the tools here) or **You** (accounts, money, people, physical steps).

## 5. Rank and pitch

Sort by impact over effort. For the top 5, add two lines on how. Include at least one bold idea. Flag any idea that clashes with the project's vibe or constraints rather than dropping it silently.

End with: "Say `/do-it-all` for the Claude items, or name the numbers you want."
